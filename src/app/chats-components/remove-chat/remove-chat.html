Remove Chat from Your List?

This will remove the conversation from your chat list only. The other participant will still be able to see the chat and its messages.

What happens when you remove this chat:

  • The chat and all its messages will be removed only for you
  • The other person will not be notified
  • You can start the chat again anytime by adding the user again
  • You will not receive notifications for new messages sent to this chat
